# IDENTITY_QUERY Review

Initial candidates: 9. Final clean families: 5. Name, direct identity, self-introduction, entity nature, and polar identity confirmation are independently useful lineages.

Merged: role/name-description requests were merged into self-introduction when they ask what Nel is. Rejected: capability questions, job/utility questions, provider/model questions, assistant-goal questions, and third-person fragments without an antecedent. They are GENERAL_CONVERSATION or CHALLENGE, not identity.

The blueprint contains 25 illustrative examples and 15 explicit hard negatives. Challenge/boundary cases: `Sən nə üçün varsan?`, `Sənin rolun nədir?`, `Sən ChatGPT-sən?`, `Sən Gemini-san?`, `O kimdir?`. The first two normally concern purpose/capability; the next two concern technical/provider provenance; the last requires context.

Language review: `varlıq` is acceptable but lower-weight; `anlat` is conversational and should receive native review against `izah et`. `Sən necə bir Aİ-san?` is deliberately not clean identity data because it mixes identity and technical classification.

Ready to freeze provisionally: yes. The main evaluation boundary is identity versus capability/provider questions.
