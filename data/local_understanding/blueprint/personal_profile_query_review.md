# PERSONAL_PROFILE_QUERY Review

Initial candidates: 9. Final clean families: 5. Direct knowledge, characterization, summary, retained-information inventory, and extent framing have distinct requested outputs. Synonymous broad-knowledge wording was merged into PPQ-01; indirect polite requests were merged into their underlying speech acts.

Rejected/challenge-only: personal-history retrieval, explicit conversation-log requests, exact storage-layer inspection, specific attribute questions, assistant identity, and public discussion of profiles or memory systems. The five families contain 25 examples and 15 hard negatives.

PFQ boundary: a question narrowed to one attribute remains PFQ even when it contains `haqqında nə bilirsən`. History boundary: `Keçən dəfə mənim haqqımda nə danışmışdıq?` is never PPQ. Memory wording: PPQ-04 permits broad retained-information language but forbids records/logs/layer-specific wording; ambiguous `yaddaşında` constructions belong in challenge data.

Runtime gap: v1 has no deterministic profile composer or disclosure policy spanning Facts, Goals, and durable MemoryService. A future service needs source precedence, scope limits, and explicit separation of ephemeral ConversationSession evidence from durable state.

Language review: `Mənim profilimi yekunlaşdır` and `məlumatlar mövcuddur` are lower-weight formal forms. PPQ-02 must not generate unsupported personality inference. Ready to freeze provisionally: yes, with PPQ-04 marked for storage-boundary evaluation.
